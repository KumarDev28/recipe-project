Simple Omelette

Tow eggs, a hot pan and, whatever is left in the fridge.

Introductions

Prep Time

Preparation: 5 minutes

Cooking: 5 minutes

Total: 10 minutes

Ingredients

2 to 3 eggs

Salt and pepper

Butter or oil

Optional fillings

Instructions

1. Beat the eggs

2. Heat the pan

3. Cook the Omelette

4. Add fillings

5. Fold and serve

Nutrition

277 kcal | 0g carbs | 20g protein | 22g fat
