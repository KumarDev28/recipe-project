Avocado Toast

Sourdough, ripe avocado, and a good squeeze of lemon.

Introductions

Prep Time

Preparation: 5 minutes

Cooking: 3 minutes

Total: 8 minutes

Ingredients

Sourdough bread

Ripe avocado

Lemon, salt, chili flakes

Olive oil, optional egg

Instructions

1. Toast the bread

2. Mash the avocado

3. Spread on toast

4. Add oil and chili flakes

5. Serve right away

Nutrition

230 kcal | 22g carbs | 6g protein | 14g fat
