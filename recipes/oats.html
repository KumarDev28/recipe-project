Overnight Oats

Stir it together tonight, wake up to breakfast.

Introductions

Prep Time

Active: 5 minutes

Chiling: 4 hours or overnight

Total: Overnight

Ingredients

Rolled oats, milk, yogurt

Honey or maple syrup

Mixed berries, cinnamon

Instructions

1. Combine the base

2. Stir well

3. Refrigerate overnight

4. Stir again

5. Top with berries and serve

Nutrition

290 kcal | 45g carbs | 12g protein | 7g fat
